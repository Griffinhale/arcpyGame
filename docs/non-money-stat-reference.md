# Non-Money Stat Reference

| Stat | Meaning | Source examples | Gameplay effects | Map visibility | Report wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Activity | Local economic activity and growth pressure. | Business permits, transit access, development approvals, active commerce features. | Raises population attraction and scenario/audit upside; can worsen displacement when vacancy or affordability is low. | `economic_growth` when growth is the leading local cause; `high_activity` as an attribute state. | Activity gain, local activity, economic growth. |
| Friction | Visible civic friction. | Public incidents, unresolved dockets, failed approvals, high-risk or hazard conditions. | Raises failure chance, population loss, audit findings, incident pressure, and citywide civic concern. | `incident` once visible; otherwise contributes through grievance and other causes; `high_friction` as an attribute state. | Civic friction, visible incident, pressure. |
| Trust | Civic trust, public value, and social cohesion. | Parks, education, public art, reserve protection, successful civic responses. | Supports retention and lightly dampens grievance drift before incidents form. | Retained as an attribute/report signal; `high_trust` as an attribute state. | Trust, cohesion, public value. |
| Exposure | Safety, infrastructure, environmental, and operational exposure. | Hazards, unsafe work, degraded features, service failures, bad land-use fit. | Raises failure chance, population loss, audit findings, hazard pressure, and incident risk. | Usually appears through `hazard` or `service_gap`; critical values remain audit-visible; `high_exposure` as an attribute state. | Safety exposure, operational exposure, hazard pressure. |
| Services | Local service capacity only. | Service facilities, network access, maintenance, utility and mobility features. | Reduces service gaps, mitigates hazards through network access, and lowers dissatisfaction pressure for affected groups. | `service_gap` when unmet demand is the leading local cause. | Service capacity, coverage gap, local service relief. |
| Dissatisfaction | Group-specific grievance band from 0 to 4. | Denials, ignored cases, hazards, service gaps, housing pressure, failed approvals. | Stays local until band 4 opens a civic incident and adds city friction once. | `grievance` at band 3; `incident` at band 4. | quiet, watching, annoyed, aggrieved, incident-ready. |

Display-state priority is `incident`, `grievance`, `service_gap`, `hazard`, `housing_pressure`, `economic_growth`, then `stable`. District type, severity, hazards, housing, network access, and stakeholder heat remain in attributes, dashboard text, and reports.

Internal-only systems include population mix, dissatisfaction/grievance bands, hazards, housing pressure, district identity, buyout pressure, maintenance condition, stakeholder heat/memory, and inspection risk bands. These may affect the public stats and audit findings, but they are not the core city-health stat model.
