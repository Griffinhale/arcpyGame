# Non-Money Stat Reference

| Stat | Meaning | Source examples | Gameplay effects | Map visibility | Report wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prosperity | Local economic activity and growth pressure. | Business permits, transit access, development approvals, active commerce features. | Raises population attraction and scenario/audit upside; can worsen displacement when vacancy or affordability is low. | `economic_growth` when growth is the leading local cause. | Economic growth, prosperity gain, local activity. |
| Unrest | Visible civic friction. | Public incidents, unresolved dockets, failed approvals, high-risk or hazard conditions. | Raises failure chance, population loss, audit findings, incident pressure, and citywide civic concern. | `incident` once visible; otherwise contributes through grievance and other causes. | Civic friction, unrest, visible incident. |
| Culture | Civic trust, public value, and social cohesion. | Parks, education, public art, reserve protection, successful civic responses. | Supports retention and lightly dampens grievance drift before incidents form. | Retained as an attribute/report signal, not a primary map cause. | Trust, cohesion, public value. |
| Risk | Safety, infrastructure, environmental, and operational exposure. | Hazards, unsafe work, degraded features, service failures, bad land-use fit. | Raises failure chance, population loss, audit findings, hazard pressure, and incident risk. | Usually appears through `hazard` or `service_gap`; critical values remain audit-visible. | Safety exposure, operational risk, hazard pressure. |
| Services | Local service capacity only. | Service facilities, network access, maintenance, utility and mobility features. | Reduces service gaps, mitigates hazards through network access, and lowers dissatisfaction pressure for affected groups. | `service_gap` when unmet demand is the leading local cause. | Service capacity, coverage gap, local service relief. |
| Dissatisfaction | Group-specific grievance band from 0 to 4. | Denials, ignored cases, hazards, service gaps, housing pressure, failed approvals. | Stays local until band 4 opens a civic incident and adds city unrest once. | `grievance` at band 3; `incident` at band 4. | quiet, watching, annoyed, aggrieved, incident-ready. |

Display-state priority is `incident`, `grievance`, `service_gap`, `hazard`, `housing_pressure`, `economic_growth`, then `stable`. District type, severity, hazards, housing, network access, and stakeholder heat remain in attributes, dashboard text, and reports.
